# arXiv subject classifications

arXiv subject classifications: their abbreviations and corresponding
descriptions.

## Usage

``` r
data(arxiv_cats)
```

## Format

A data frame with five columns: the abbreviations of the subject
classifications (`category`), the field of study, subfield of study
(within Physics; `NA` otherwise), a short description, and a longer
description.

## Source

<https://arxiv.org/category_taxonomy>
